Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start automating your workflows and building your web presence.

What services do you offer?

×

I offer end-to-end business process automation using Power Automate, Make.com, and RPA, along with responsive React and PCF frontend development.

How do I get started working with you?

+

Just reach out through the contact form or a free consultation call. We'll discuss your workflow or project, and I'll map out a clear plan before any work begins.

Do you work with businesses of any size?

+

Yes — from solo founders automating a single process to growing teams needing multiple connected systems, the approach scales to fit your needs.

Can we work together remotely?

+

Absolutely. All work, communication, and delivery happens remotely — calls, messages, and shared files, wherever you're based.

How quickly can automation be set up?

×

Most workflows are designed, tested, and deployed within one to two weeks, depending on how many systems need to connect.

Do I need to change my existing tools?

+

No — automations are built around the tools you already use, connecting them rather than replacing them.

What happens if a flow breaks after launch?

+

Every flow includes error handling, and support is available afterward to fix and refine anything that needs adjusting.

Which platforms do you automate?

+

Power Automate, Power Platform, Make.com, and RPA for desktop-based or legacy systems are all covered.

Do you build fully responsive websites?

×

Yes — every site is built to work smoothly across desktop, tablet, and mobile from the start.

Can you convert my Figma design into code?

+

Yes, pixel-perfect Figma-to-code conversion is one of the core frontend services offered.

Do you build custom PCF components for Power Apps?

+

Yes — custom Power Apps Component Framework controls tailored to your app's exact workflow and data.

How is pricing determined?

×

Pricing depends on project scope — number of workflows, systems involved, and frontend complexity. A clear quote is shared after the initial discovery call.

What's your typical project timeline?

+

Smaller automations or single-page builds typically take one to two weeks; larger projects are scoped and timelined individually.

Do you require payment upfront?

+

A partial deposit is required to begin work, with the remaining balance due on delivery.

Do you offer ongoing support after delivery?

×

Yes — post-launch support is available to fix issues, adjust flows, or make small updates as your needs change.

How can I reach you for urgent issues?

+

Through email or the contact form — urgent issues are prioritized and responded to as quickly as possible.

Do you provide documentation for automations?

+

Yes — every automation comes with a simple written overview of how it works, so your team isn't left guessing.
